## Standing rules (carried from this cycle's lessons)

1. **HR governs pace.** Every tempo/interval: conservative km1/rep1, watch alert on the fast limit. The km2/rep1 blowup derailed every quality session this cycle — do not repeat.
2. **Jog recoveries, never walk** — keeps HR elevated into Zone 5 on intervals (the 4/12 Jun lesson).
3. **Chest-strap HR for the rehearsal and race** — optical watch undercounts ~25–35 bpm in races (8 Mar, 27 Jun).
4. **Illness = stop.** Any cough/phlegm/sore throat → complete rest. This is what cost the original race.
5. **Heat:** late-July London can be hot — if race day >22°C, run on HR/effort and accept a slower target.
