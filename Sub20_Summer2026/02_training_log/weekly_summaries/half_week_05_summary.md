   faster race is therefore a shorter race** — 4:50 for 1:42 is less exposure
   than 5:05 for 1:47.
3. **No chest strap, no aerobic verdict.** Saturday's wrist optical reported
   4:26/km at 148 bpm. Sunday's strap reported 4:42/km at 165. Saturday was
   under-reading by ~25 bpm and is disregarded.
