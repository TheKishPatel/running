## What the week established

**1. The long-run creep is broken.** The defining pattern of the campaign, corrected.

| Segment | 10 Aug (14 km) | **16 Aug (16 km)** |
|---|---|---|
| First third | 5:44 | 5:45 |
| Middle | 5:33 | 5:42 |
| Final third of body | 5:30 | 5:40 |
| **Drift** | **14 sec/km** | **5 sec/km** |

On 10 Aug the drift, not the distance, pushed HR to the cap (max 155, 0:54 above 150). On 16 Aug the body of the run held and **HR at km13 was 147** — which is what made the finish available.

**2. Half-marathon race pace is demonstrably in the legs.** km14–16 at **4:51 / 4:52 / 4:54, HR 161 / 160 / 162, power 249 / 246 / 243 w**, off 13 km of prior running.

| Reference | Value |
|---|---|
| Threshold power | 271–281 w |
| **Race-pace segment** | **246 w ≈ 89% of threshold** |
| Threshold HR band | 168–176 |
| **Race-pace segment HR** | **161** |
| Zone 4 time | **0:00** |

Race pace at a genuinely sub-threshold cost. The strongest single indicator of the block.

**3. The gate process worked.** The 48 h soreness reading was confounded by Barry's the day before, so the gate fell to a functional test — Wednesday's 5 km. It returned **133 avg dead centre of the predicted 132–136 band, flat across 30 minutes at 200–202 w, and a 44 bpm first-minute recovery.** Heaviness was local DOMS, not systemic fatigue, and 16 km was correctly cleared.

**4. Rest was taken correctly under pressure.** Thursday's resting HR of 62 with a badly broken night triggered the >60 rule on the hardest session of the block. **The VO2max was dropped rather than rolled to Friday**, protecting the Sunday. Resting HR came back to **49 by Sunday — lowest of the campaign** — confirming sleep, not illness.

**5. Two targets were corrected off measured data.** VO2max **4:02 → 4:08** (the 4:02 was a legacy number from the abandoned sub-20 goal; threshold is flat at 4:23 and good-conditions 5K pace is 4:08–4:10). Reps cut 5 → 4 to protect the 16 km.

**6. Athlete record corrected.** The repo claimed **no half-marathon experience**; the athlete has completed **5–6 halves and used gels before**. 16 km was the longest run of *this campaign*, not ever. The remaining gap to 6 Sep is time on feet, not unfamiliarity — the race risk profile is lower than the readiness table implied.
