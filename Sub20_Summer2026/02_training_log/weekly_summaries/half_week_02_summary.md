## What the week established

**1. Barry's intensity corrected.** 28 Jul was too light (125 avg, 32% Zone 2–3). 4 Aug returned to profile: **135 avg, Zone 2–3 at 64% of a 41:44 session, Zone 4 zero.** No third hard day created.

**2. Threshold has not moved in five weeks — the block's central assumption did not hold.**

| | 2 Jul | 6 Aug |
|---|---|---|
| Main set | 4 km @ 4:20 | 4 km @ **4:23** |
| HR | mean ~170 | **169** |
| Power | 271–281 w | **272–275 w** |

Three sec/km slower at 1 bpm lower and equal power. Execution shape was right (4:26 → 4:23 → 4:21, descending, no early spike) but **he finished with headroom — 169 against a 178 ease-threshold.**

**3. First under-target session of the campaign.** Every prior flag was overspeed. This was the reverse and read as over-correction after three weeks of slow-down instruction. Distinction recorded: pace discipline governs easy runs and the opening rep; on the body of a quality set the pace is the target and HR is the ceiling.

**4. The data-capture problem was found and fixed.** A 1.5 km warm-up put half of rep 1 inside watch-km2 and left every later split straddling two intensities. **`CLAUDE.md` now mandates whole-kilometre warm-ups, and `data_capture_protocol.md` was created.** From 6 Aug, structured watch workouts are standard for multi-intensity sessions — and worked first time.

**5. Resting HR measurement rebuilt.** A 59 reading on 5 Aug triggered a diagnostic easy run (139 @ 5:46, post-run 131→96→96) which cleared it. On 6 Aug the strap would not settle at all (52 → mid-60s), diagnosed as dry electrodes plus respiratory sinus arrhythmia rather than physiology. **Protocol v2: wet contacts, settle 2 minutes, record a 60-second average with the range.**

**6. Heat management entered the block.** Sunday peaked at 32°C. The morning window was the only usable one and was missed, so the long run moved to Monday rather than being run hot — 16:30 at 30°C over 18 km being a heat-illness exposure, not a slow run.
