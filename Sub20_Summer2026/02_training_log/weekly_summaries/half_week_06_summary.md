**The watch's 21.413 km in 1:41:01 reconciles to the official time exactly:** the 0.316 km
over-measure is worth 89 seconds at the run's own pace, and 6061 − 89 = 5972. Both sources
give 4:43.1/km.

### The plan was not followed, and it still worked

| Segment | Prescribed | Actual |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 km | **4:55/km** | **4:39/km — 16 sec/km fast** |
| 5–15 km | 4:50/km | 4:40/km |
| 15–21.1 km | 4:45/km | 4:51/km |

**Rule 1 — "never faster than 4:50 in the first 5 km; if km1 is under 4:45, slow down
immediately" — was broken inside the first kilometre.** km1 ran 4:40.

**The campaign's signature failure mode was executed in full and the engine was big enough
to carry it.** That is not a vindication: the 4:55 → 4:50 → 4:45 shape was designed to
produce a *negative* split and the result was a 16 sec/km positive one. **It is a real
fade, not a course artifact — power fell 12.3 W while heart rate rose 7.5 bpm.** Had the
back half simply been hillier, power would have held and pace dropped.

### Three signals he was fitter than the result

1. **There was a finishing gear, so this was not a wall.** km21 ran **4:42 at 254 W — the
   highest power of the whole second half** — and the closing 413 m ran **4:36**.
2. **He held 4:35/km for 10 km at 166.6 bpm — below the 168–176 threshold band.**
3. **The 30 Aug final read understated him.** The race ran 7 sec/km faster than that read
   for 3.5× the duration, and the read came on a five-waking night 48 h after a maximal 5 km.

**Estimate: a disciplined 4:45 open is most likely 1:38:30–1:39:00.** Better pacing was
worth ~30–60 seconds here, not minutes — the result sits close to the honest ceiling.

## What the block delivered

**The long-run ramp landed every rung: 12 → 14 → 16 → 18, with no 21 km training run by
design.** The peak (18.01 km, 23 Aug) passed with the HR cap never touched — max 145
against a <150 cap, Zone 3/4/5 all zero.

**The volume experiment is answered, and it was the campaign's real open question.** The 5K
campaign died at 4:00/km with threshold never below 4:20 and volume at 20–25 km/week; **low
volume was named as the untested ceiling and this block tested it.** 21.1 km at 4:43/km
settles it — **the aerobic base is no longer the limiter.**

**What the block did not deliver: threshold progression.** 4:20/km (2 Jul) → 4:23/km
(6 Aug) → nothing, across three consecutive compromised Thursdays — under-pressed, RHR 62,
knee injury. **Stated as a settled fact throughout, and it remains one. That bill is now
due to the October 5K.**
