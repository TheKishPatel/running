## What the week established

### 1. The 18 km gate passed on every criterion, with room to spare

| Criterion | Required | Delivered |
|---|---|---|
| Distance | 18 km | **18.01 km** |
| HR | <150 cap | **132 avg, max 145 — never touched** |
| No collapse km15–18 | — | **5:47.8 vs 5:45.4 body = 2.4 sec/km** |

Every previous long run reached or breached its cap (2 Aug 152, 10 Aug 155, 16 Aug 170). This one finished with 5 bpm in hand and **zero seconds above Zone 2.**

### 2. The creep is gone, not merely broken

| Quarter | Pace | HR | Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| km3–6 | 5:45.2 | 126 | 210 w |
| km7–10 | 5:45.2 | 132 | 210 w |
| km11–14 | 5:43.5 | 137 | 211 w |
| km15–18 | 5:47.8 | 138 | 208 w |

**4.3 sec/km range across 18 km, last quarter slowest.** Against 14 sec/km (10 Aug) and 5 sec/km (16 Aug). Fastest km was 5:38 — the 5:35 floor held.

### 3. Aerobic efficiency is the best of the campaign

**km3–18: 5:45.4/km @ 133.1 bpm, 209.6 w → 1.575 w/bpm**, against **1.529** for the 16 Aug body (5:42 @ 138.6, 212 w). **16 km cost less per beat than 12 km did a week earlier — +3.0%.**

**Counterweight: decoupling 6.2%** (EF 1.628 → 1.526), above the 5% threshold, against 4.3% on the 12 km of 2 Aug. Longer runs decouple more and 1 h 44 in late-August London carries thermal load. The drift was purely HR at constant power and pace.

### 4. Injury management worked

A non-training traction injury on Wednesday could have ended the block. It cost one threshold session and nothing else:
- Threshold **dropped, not rolled to Friday** — the pre-written rule decided it before the moment arrived
- Test **deferred 12 h** to Friday morning to buy healing time
- Six graded functional steps gated the run; the run gated the long run
- **The original medial structure stayed silent across all 18 km**

### 5. ⚠️ One new flag out of the peak

**Last 4 km: lateral knee + dorsal right forefoot (4th/5th MTP). Resolved inside 24 h.** Fatigue-dependent gait compensation around the healing medial structure. Yellow, not red — but Week 5 puts race pace back in twice.
