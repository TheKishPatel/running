**Race:** Sunday 6 September 2026, half marathon (21.1 km), London.

**Base at start:** best aerobic efficiency of the campaign (5 Jul: 5:19/km @ 142 BPM); threshold 4:20/km clean (2 Jul); 5K fitness ~20:45–21:00 in good conditions.

## The problem this block solves

| Factor | Status at 25 Jul |
|--------|------------------|
| Aerobic efficiency | **Strong** — 5:19/km @ 142 BPM |
| Threshold | 4:20/km for 4 km, clean descending shape |
| **Longest run ever logged** | **11.0 km** (7 Jun 2026) |
| Runs per week, last 6 weeks | 2–3 |
| Actual weekly volume, last 6 weeks | 15–24 km |
| Half-marathon distance experience | **None** |

The engine is fine. The endurance is not there. The half is 21.1 km and the athlete has never run past 11 — not "not recently", never, across the entire Oct 2025 – Jul 2026 record. Planned peak volume last block was 35–40 km/week; actual was roughly half that, gutted twice by illness.

**This block builds the volume the sub-20 campaign never had.** That is not a detour from the 5K goal — the 5K campaign died at 4:00/km with threshold never below 4:20 and volume at 20–25 km/week. Low volume is the most plausible ceiling that has never been tested.

---

## Goals

- **Primary (realistic): 1:42–1:45** — 4:50–4:58/km.
- **Stretch, if the block runs clean: 1:40** — 4:44/km.
- **Race target pace: 4:50/km.** All half-marathon-pace work in this block is run at 4:50.
- **Do nothing different and just show up:** ~1:50+, with the last 5 km falling apart. Not the plan.

**Basis:** Riegel off the 20:59 PB gives 1:36:30, but that assumes an endurance base that does not exist. Threshold at 4:20/km puts half-marathon pace at threshold + 25–30 sec/km given the thin base → 4:45–4:55. 1:42 is the honest number.

### Secondary goal — the 5K does not stop

Threshold is the primary engine for both distances, and this block trains it. What gets parked is 5–6 weeks of top-end VO2max sharpness, which returns in 3 weeks. Aerobic base takes months.

**Route: half (6 Sep) → 4-week 5K sharpening block → cool-weather 5K, early-to-mid October.** Today's 21:27 was a 24°C tax on ~20:45 fitness; October gives that back for free, off a much bigger base. The road to sub-20 goes *through* the half, not around it.

---

## Standing rules for this block

1. **No third hard day.** Tuesday Barry's + Thursday quality is already two. The Sunday long run stays strictly aerobic, **HR <150, no exceptions**. The moment it becomes an effort it is a third hard day plus Barry's, and the record says what happens next.
2. **On runs ≥14 km, slower is correct.** The recurring pattern is running 10–15 sec/km faster than prescribed. On short runs the HR data has excused it. On a 16–18 km run it does not — drifting fast buys nothing and costs the following week's quality session. **HR 150 is a hard cap, not a target.**
3. **Illness = full stop.** Four respiratory episodes plus food poisoning in this campaign. Volume is going up ~60%, which raises risk. Sore throat, cough or phlegm → complete rest, per `CLAUDE.md`. A week lost in weeks 1–3 is recoverable; a week lost in weeks 4–5 means racing untrained for the distance.
4. **Chest strap on every long run and on race day.** Three races now with no usable HR. The long runs need a real governor and the habit needs building before September.
5. **Fuel the long runs.** The 16 km and 18 km runs are the first runs over 90 minutes ever. Gel at 45 min and 75 min, practised on both. Carbs 338–405 g on those days per `CLAUDE.md`.
6. **Barry's stays.** Consistent lower-body work is what holds the legs together through an 18 km run — the 31 Mar VMO twinge is the evidence for what a gap costs. Only modify plyometrics if the legs are already heavily fatigued.
7. **Volume held, not chased.** 40 km in week 4 is more than has ever actually been run. If week 3 or 4 feels wrong — HR up at easy pace, motivation gone — **hold volume flat rather than progressing.** The table is a plan, not a debt.

## Race-day pacing

**Target 1:42 (4:50/km). Negative split. The entire failure history is aggressive opens — 4:04 parkrun (Feb), gate rep 3 (16 Jul), 3 of 4 reps hot (23 Jul).**

| Segment | Pace | Cumulative | Cue |
|---------|------|------------|-----|
| 0–5 km | **4:55/km** | 24:35 | "Too easy. That's correct." |
| 5–15 km | **4:50/km** | 1:13:00 | Lock effort. Refuse the mid-race sag — that cost a km on 25 Jul. |
| 15–21.1 km | **4:45/km or whatever is left** | **~1:42** | Spend it. |

**Rules:**
1. **Never faster than 4:50 in the first 5 km.** If km 1 comes in under 4:45, slow down immediately.
2. If 5 km takes 5:05/km — do not panic, that is a 1:47 and still a good day. Settle.
3. Heat contingency: >22°C, add 8–10 sec/km and run on HR. The 25 Jul lesson is that heat is a real tax, not a mental one.
4. Fuel: gel at 45 min and 75 min, same as the long runs. Water at every station from 5 km.
