|-----------|--------|

| Main set | **5×1000m @ 4:02/km, 90s JOG recovery** |
| Cooldown | 1km @ 5:45/km |
| Total | ~7.5km |

- **Rep pace 4:02/km — rep 1 at 4:02, NOT faster.** Watch alert at 4:00/km (fast limit). First rep should feel ~7/10 (almost easy); last rep wretched.
- **Jog the recoveries, never walk.** The two prior interval sessions (4 Jun walked to <140; 12 Jun DNF) never reached Zone 5. Jog keeps HR ~150–160 between reps so it accumulates into Zone 5 by rep 3–4.

- Completing 5 clean reps with jog recovery matters more than chasing 3:58 — this is a stimulus, not a test. Do not "test" fitness on rep 1 (the recurring early-rep blowup).
