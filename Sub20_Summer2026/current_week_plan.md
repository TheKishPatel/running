### Targets

**Why 4:20 and not 4:15:** threshold was 4:20 (2 Jul) and 4:23 (6 Aug). Since then: a half
marathon, 8 days off, a tendon flag, and one easy week. **4:15 would be a target set by the goal,
not by the data.** The data says 4:20 is the honest re-entry. **If 4:20 comes in low in the band,
W3 moves it. That is the aggressive-adaptation rule working as intended.**

**Why 4 × 1 km and not 4 km continuous:** the same 4 km at threshold as 6 Aug, in four readable
pieces. **Rep 1 vs rep 4 at the same pace is the drift read.** And 90 s breaks cut the
continuous force on a tendon 16 days clear, at almost no cost to the stimulus.

**Why a built workout:** easy runs are now governed by terrain. **A threshold cannot be.**
The hard-end precedent is **27 Aug: 3 km @ 4:50/km prescribed, a maximal 5 km @ 4:29/km run.**
A watch alert that buzzes at 4:15 is the only mechanism in this campaign that has not yet failed.
