## 💪 TENDON LOADING PROGRAMME — RUNS ALONGSIDE, NOT INSTEAD OF

**Unchanged from 13 Sep and it is not optional.** Tendons remodel in response to graded load;
they do not heal with rest.

- **Stage 1 — isometric single-leg heel raise, hold at mid-height: 5 × 30–45 s. DAILY.**
- **Stage 2 — slow heavy single-leg heel raise, 3 s up / 3 s down, full range: 3 × 10–15.
  EVERY OTHER DAY.** This is the main remodelling stimulus.
- **Stage 3 — add band inversion, 3 × 15**, once stage 2 is comfortable. **Given Monday's clean
  read, add it this week.**
- **Judgement is the next morning**, not during. Soreness ≤3/10 during is expected and fine.
  Worse on waking → drop back a stage.

**Do not skip days waiting for it to feel perfect. That is the failure mode that produced the
13 Sep setback.**
