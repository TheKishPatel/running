**km2 → km5: pace +28 sec/km, HR +9 bpm, power −28 w (−9.9%), efficiency
−14.4%.**

Rising heart rate against falling power at a slowing pace is decoupling in its
plainest form. Sustainable output was exceeded from km2, and the last three
kilometres were paid for out of the first two.

**km1 went out at 4:22 from cold with no warm-up** — HR 152 for that split is
the lag, not the cost. This is the aggressive-open failure mode written into
the race plan, expressed in its purest available form. It is worth naming
precisely because 6 Sep is decided by the opposite behaviour.

**The re-laced shoe cleared under maximum force.** The athlete reports no
issue. This is a far stronger test than the prescribed easy run would have

## 3. What the 5 km time does and does not say

**22:28 does not read on sub-20.** No warm-up, a heavily positive split,
mid-half-block, and no speed work since 2 Jul.

Warmed up, evenly paced and rested, this body projects to roughly
**21:15–21:45** today — about 90 seconds off the October target. That is the
most useful 5 km datapoint the campaign has produced, and it is the October
project's real starting line.

**It is not a half-marathon signal in either direction.** 16 Aug (3 km @
4:52/km, 161 bpm, 246 w off 13 km) and 23 Aug (18.01 km, 132 avg, max 145)
remain the evidence for 6 Sep.

**The threshold read is not clean.** km3 ran 4:24 @ 179 against 6 Aug's 4:23
@ 169 — the same pace for 10 bpm more. But that came off a 4:14 km2 with no
warm-up, in the evening, on a 56 morning reading. **Do not conclude threshold
has declined from this.**

## 4. The cost, and the decision it forces

HR was **≥174 from km2 to the finish** — roughly 18 minutes at Zone 4 or
above, peaking at 187. A genuine maximal 5 km carries a 2–3 day cost, and
Sunday morning sits **~60 hours out**, inside that window.

**DECISION — Sunday's race-pace block trims 6 km → 4 km.** Total stays 14 km,
structure unchanged: **5 km easy / 4 km @ 4:50 / 5 km easy.**

The read survives. **4 km at 4:50 still exceeds the 3 km that produced the
16 Aug finding** — it is a shorter measurement, not a lost one. Saturday
remains a 5 km shakeout; 3 km or nothing if the legs are heavy.
