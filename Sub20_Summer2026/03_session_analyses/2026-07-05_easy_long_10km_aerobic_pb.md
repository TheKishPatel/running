## Pace-discipline note (why this isn't a flag)

Main set 5:19/km is 16 sec/km faster than the 5:35 target, which on paper trips the
CLAUDE.md ">15 sec/km too fast" easy-run flag. It does not apply here. That flag
exists to catch easy pace *dragging HR up out of the aerobic zone*. HR never left
Zone 2 (max 150, Zone 3+ = 0:00). The pace was a downstream product of improved
fitness at a controlled HR, not a discipline slip. HR was the governor and it held —
which is exactly the doctrine.

The distinction to hold onto: this is fine **because HR stayed put**. If a future
easy run runs this fast *and* HR creeps toward or past 150, that is the real flag —
back off pace immediately.

Recovery: 141→105→93 BPM in 2 min — excellent parasympathetic response.
