## The HR is not usable — read via power

Optical watch was used despite the chest-strap mandate. Per the established race-day pattern (8 Mar, 27 Jun), optical **undercounts 25–35 bpm on hard efforts**. So:
- The 156/162/165 rep averages and the "Zone 3 dominant" distribution are **not trustworthy** and are **NOT an efficiency read** (running fast at "low" HR is a sensor artifact, not fitness).
- Max **180 (1 bpm under the Z5 line)** plus the retch reflex confirms he was **at his ceiling** on rep 3 — consistent with the athlete's own report that he could not have gone faster.

**Power is the honest, HR-independent signal:** reps **288 / 287 / 279 W** = at/above the 2 Jul threshold power (271–281 W), fading on rep 3. **At threshold-plus wattage he produced 4:07–4:18, not 4:00.** The speed for a clean 4:00/km is not there.

But the plan's own rule stands: a rough gate isn't a fitness verdict, yet **race-day pacing goes off what the gate shows.** Pace conservative; let him outrun it.
