## Why this one matters

The recurring flag all campaign has been the **km2 blowup**: five straight tempos
where rep 2 went 4–9 sec/km too fast, spiked HR, and derailed the session (27 Mar,
2 Apr, 23 Apr, 7 May, 30 May). Root cause logged as pace instinct being too
aggressive once warm.

This session inverted that. T1 was the **slowest** rep (4:27), and pace descended
monotonically to a 4:13 close. HR followed the same clean staircase —
156→171→175→179 — with no premature spike. That is the pacing discipline we've
been trying to install, executed for the first time.

Power confirms the control: 271–272w held flat across T1–T3, stepping up to 281w
only on the deliberate final push.

## Caveats (minor, not faults)

- **T4 at 4:13/km breached the 4:16 fast limit** by 3 sec/km, and its 179 BPM nudged
  the "ease to 4:24 if HR >178" threshold. As a controlled last-rep fast finish this
  is acceptable — max HR only touched 182, no runaway. The discipline note for next
  time: keep 4:13 as the *closing* rep, never the opener.
- Cooldown slow at 6:06 — irrelevant.
