## Why Zone 5 Wasn't Reached

Two compounding factors:

1. **Pace 5-8 sec/km below target.** 4:02/km = 3:14 per rep. At 4:08/km = 3:18 per rep — 4 seconds longer per rep, less cardiovascular stress per unit time. At target pace, HR would peak higher.

2. **Walking to <140 BPM between reps.** Each rep started from a fully recovered base (~138-140 BPM). With 90s jog recovery, reps would start from ~155-160 BPM — 15-20 BPM higher — and HR would breach 181 BPM much earlier in each rep. Walking recovery diluted the cumulative HR stimulus across the session.

These two factors combined kept max HR at 178 (just short of Zone 5 at 181+) and session avg at 157 BPM.
