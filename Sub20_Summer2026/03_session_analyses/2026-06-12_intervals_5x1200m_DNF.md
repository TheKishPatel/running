## Why this is not a fitness read

Four independent stressors stacked on one session:

1. **Phlegm + 2-week nasal drip** — active low-grade respiratory illness. Per CLAUDE.md this is a complete-rest trigger; the session should not have been run hard.
2. **GI upset Tue 9 Jun** — likely lingering under-fuelling/under-recovery.
3. **Broken sleep** — child woke 3×. History shows ~10 sec/km cost on quality work from poor sleep alone (14 May tempo).
4. **Low motivation** — nearly skipped the run.

Any one degrades a VO2max session; together the result carries no fitness signal.

## The data argues *against* a fitness ceiling

- **Submaximal HR at the stop.** Rep averages 163/163/171 vs a Zone 5 floor of 181. Even with HR lag, R3 likely peaked ~178–180 — he stopped before maxing the cardiovascular system. A true fitness limit looks like HR pinned at 181+ with pace collapsing; neither occurred.
- **Reps accelerated** (4:11 → 4:09 → 4:06) rather than fading — the opposite of aerobic failure.
- **Power steady** at 286–288W across all three reps.
- **Recovery controlled** — jogged (not walked), HR held 163–166.

High perceived effort + moderate HR = central/illness/fuel limiter, not peripheral fitness. The legs were running near target; the session ended on the head under load.

## Corroborating fitness evidence (recent, clean)

- 7 Jun long run: 5:30/km @ 140 BPM — best aerobic efficiency of the campaign.
- Today's reps at 4:06–4:11 sat only 4–9 sec off a 4:02 target *while sick and underslept*.

The aerobic base is intact. The unknown remains the top-end VO2max/threshold ceiling — which illness has repeatedly prevented reading cleanly.
