### What the soreness check actually is

The gate reads *"no soreness lasting beyond 48 h."* Soreness in the 24–48 h window after the
longest run you have ever done is **expected and means nothing**. The question is whether it has
**cleared, or is clearly clearing, by 48 hours** — that is the difference between a ramp the body
is absorbing and a ramp that is outrunning it.

**Complication: Tuesday's Barry's lands in the middle of the window** and will produce its own
soreness, peaking Wednesday morning — exactly when the 48 h reading is due. So take three
readings, not one:

| When | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Tue 11 Aug, on waking — before Barry's** | 24 h read on the run alone, uncontaminated |
| **Wed 12 Aug, on waking** | 48 h read, confounded — attribute by location (below) |
| **Wed 12 Aug, first 2 km of the easy run** | The functional test, and the most reliable of the three |

**Attribution by location.** The 14 km loads **calves, achilles, hamstrings, glutes**. Barry's
lower-body loads **quads and glutes** via squats and lunges. Quad soreness on Wednesday is almost
certainly Barry's. **Calf or achilles soreness on Wednesday is the run**, and that is the one that
matters.

**How to read it:**
- **Diffuse, both legs, dull, fading** → normal adaptation. Passes even if faintly present at 48 h.
- **Calf or achilles still sore at 48 h, not improving** → `CLAUDE.md` red flag: the volume ramp is
  too fast. **Repeat 14 km on Sun 16 Aug instead of progressing to 16.**
- **Sharp, localised, one-sided, or worsening** → that is not soreness, it is an injury signal.
  Stop and report it; different rules apply.

works if the first 13 km are held at target rather than drifting toward it.

## HR-for-pace — drift, not decline

At matched pace *and* matched distance-in: **km11 today ran 5:28 @ 150; 2 Aug's km11 ran
5:28 @ 145.** Five beats higher for identical pace at the identical point of the run.

Some of that is the extra 2 km and a warmer morning, and the early kilometres are level
(km6 5:30 @ 137 today vs 2 Aug km8 5:29 @ 138). **Read as normal drift over a longer run, not
a fitness signal in either direction.** km14 at 6:00 @ 143 was a deliberate ease-down and is
